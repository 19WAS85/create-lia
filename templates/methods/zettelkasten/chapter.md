## Organization Method: Zettelkasten

This workspace organizes knowledge as a Zettelkasten: linked atomic notes, not folders.

- `inbox/` — fleeting notes captured before processing.
- `notes/` — permanent notes, one idea each, in the user's own words: `notes/<topic>.md`.
- `notes/index.md` — the Map of Content (MOC): navigation entries for notes worth finding again.
- `sources/` — literature notes summarizing sources with references: `sources/<topic>.md`.

### Principles

- One idea per note, written in the user's own words rather than copied.
- Link liberally to related notes; structure emerges from links, not from folders.
- Promote a fleeting note from `inbox/` into a permanent note when the idea earns its place; otherwise discard it.
- Add an entry to the Map of Content when a permanent note is worth finding again.
- Source notes capture literature together with its references.

### Task Tracking

Tasks are secondary in this method. [`TASKS.md`](TASKS.md) has two sections, `Open` and `Done`. Register only concrete pending actions; on completion, move the item to `Done` with the date. Reference information belongs in notes, not in tasks.
